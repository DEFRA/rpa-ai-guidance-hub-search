# Field Visit Guidance

This guidance explains how to plan, carry out and record a field visit. It is
invented for tests.

## 1 Planning a visit

Read [preparing evidence](#11-preparing-evidence) and the
[payment windows](#a1-payment-windows) before you book a visit. The booking
service is at [the visits portal](HTTPS://Visits.Example.COM:443/book?type=field#start).

### 1.1 Preparing evidence

Gather the following before you travel:

- the holding map, printed at a readable scale
- the previous visit log, if there is one
- a note of any [open query]{.red} or [agreed change]{.blue}
  on the case

1. Check the weather.
2. Tell the claimant when you will arrive.

| Item              | Needed for        | Notes                          |
| ----------------- | ----------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Holding map       | Every visit       | Print it<br>and bring a spare  |
| Measuring wheel   | Boundary checks   | Wheel \| tape both fine        |
| Camera            | Evidence          | Date &amp; time stamp on       |

#### 1.1.1 Photographs

Photographs must show the date. Write <u>never</u> crop a photograph, and
label each one with the field reference<sup>1</sup> and the grid square<sub>2</sub>.

## 2 Carrying out a visit

Introduce yourself and explain the visit.\
Show your identity card if asked.

Escaped characters stay as typed: 5 \* 3, \[draft\] and \_note\_ are literal,
and so are &lt;angle brackets&gt;.

### 2.1 Recording what you find
